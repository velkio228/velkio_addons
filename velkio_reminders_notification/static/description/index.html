EVERYDAY CAPABILITIES

See what the app helps you do

01

On-screen notices

Popups appear over the Odoo workspace, so readers can respond without opening a separate message list.

02

Personal and team reminders

Internal users create reminders for themselves. Notification Managers can send to selected people or everyone.

03

Snooze and acknowledgement

Allow readers to snooze a notice, or require acknowledgement. Required acknowledgement disables snooze and auto-close.

04

Dashboard and calendar

Review today’s reminders, scheduled notices, assigned items and unread messages. Open the calendar to see upcoming work.

05

Templates and popup designs

Start with the 39 bundled message templates and choose one of six popup styles. Adjust the wording for your team.

06

Multi-session response tracking

Delivery and response state are shared across sessions for the same account. Reopening Odoo lets the browser catch up on pending notices.

CONTROLS & SCOPE

Choose the right controls for your workflow

01

Everyone

Create and manage your personal reminders, read received notices and use the dashboard.

02

Notification Manager

Send notices to other people or everyone and review delivery records through Configuration.

03

Popup behavior

Choose Information, Important or Urgent. Configure sound, auto-close, acknowledgement and snooze as supported.

Understand the scope

On-screen delivery runs inside a signed-in Odoo browser session. Optional operating-system notifications depend on browser support and permission. This app does not send SMS, WhatsApp messages or mobile push notifications. Network delays and sleeping devices can affect delivery time.

GETTING STARTED

Install, configure and validate

Before installation

  • Odoo 17 with the standard base, mail and web modules.
  • A deployment that supports custom addons, such as on-premise or Odoo.sh.
  • An internal user account; assign Notification Manager to users who send team notices.
  • Keep the scheduled dispatch action enabled. Browser notification permission is optional.

Odoo Online does not support installation of custom Python addons. Choose a deployment that permits custom modules.

01

Install the app

Add the module to your addons path, restart Odoo, update the Apps list and install Velkio Reminders & Notifications.

02

Choose who can send

In Settings → Users, grant Notification Manager to the people responsible for team notices.

03

Create a reminder

Open Notifications → My Reminders. Enter a title, message and Show At time, or start from a template.

04

Set delivery options

Choose the audience and level. Set a popup style and decide whether acknowledgement, snooze or sound is appropriate.

05

Schedule and check

Schedule a short test for yourself. Keep Odoo open and check that the popup appears and responds correctly.

06

Review your day

Use Dashboard, Calendar and Sent to Me to follow upcoming reminders and received notices.

TROUBLESHOOTING

When something needs attention

01

No popup yet?

Check the Show At time, notice status, intended account and active Odoo session. Confirm the scheduled dispatch action is enabled.

02

Cannot send to others?

Check whether the sender belongs to Notification Manager. Personal reminders do not require that group.

03

No desktop notification or sound?

Check browser permission and sound settings. Browser notification support and user interaction requirements vary.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Check the details before installing

01

Who can send team notices?

Notification Managers can send to selected internal users or everyone. Other internal users can create personal reminders.

02

Does it work with a closed browser?

The on-screen popup requires an Odoo browser session. Pending notices can be recovered when Odoo is opened again.

03

Can a required notice be snoozed?

No. Requiring acknowledgement disables snooze and auto-close for that notice.

04

Is the module free?

Yes. The module is distributed under LGPL-3. Hosting and any installation services are separate.
