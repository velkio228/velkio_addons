Why This Exists

Odoo can tell people things. It cannot make them look.

Activities sit in a list. Emails wait in an inbox. Chatter needs someone to open the record. None of that helps when something has to be seen now. Velkio Reminders & Notifications puts a real popup on the screen — a genuine on-screen alert, not another line item.

⏳
On the exact second
The browser counts the time down itself, then confirms with the server before showing anything — not “within a minute”.
📱
On every screen
One account on a laptop and a phone gets the notice on both. Answer it on either and it comes down on the other.
🔔
Outside Odoo too
Optionally mirrored into the operating system’s own notification centre, so it lands even when Odoo is not the window in front.

Inside The App

Everything waiting, at a glance

📊 Dashboard — seven buckets, counted live

Today, Scheduled, Assigned to me, Flagged, Urgent, Completed, All — each one opens straight into the exact set that was counted.

📅 Calendar — your own reminders, by date

A month view of everything coming to you, colour-coded by urgency and filterable by level and author.

👁 Read tracking — know what was actually seen

Every row carries a read marker that belongs to you alone. Unread rows stay bold, with a blue dot — like unread mail.

📝 Templates — 39 notices already written

Meetings, approvals, deadlines, payroll, stock, maintenance and more. Pick one, set the time, send it.
🎨 Styles — design the popup, no code

Everything about how a notice looks lives in a record, with a “Preview on my screen” button.
💬 Form — the message, not the machinery

Open a notice somebody sent you and you get the title, when it is due, how urgent it is, who wrote it and what it says — nothing else.

Built For A Real Working Day

The small things that make it usable

📦
A backlog never floods you
Away for three days? One card appears — the most urgent — and the rest wait behind a “more waiting” chip.
⌛
Snooze that holds everywhere
Ten minutes, an hour, tomorrow. Put it off on one screen and it is put off on all of them.
✅
Acknowledgement when it matters
Some notices need an answer. Those keep coming back until somebody clicks “Got it”.
⭐
Flags that are yours
Star anything to find it again. Nobody else sees your stars, even on a notice you both received.
🔇
A quiet switch
Anyone can turn popups off for themselves. Urgent notices still get through.
👀
Nothing shown twice
A notice already answered is never pushed again. A closed device catches up the moment it opens.
Always In View

A count you cannot miss

Unread reminders are counted on the bell in the top bar and badged on the app icon, updating the moment a notice arrives — no waiting for a refresh.
Who Can Do What

Everyone reminds themselves. Managers remind the team.

Anyone
Sets reminders for themselves, stars and reads what arrives, snoozes, and answers. They cannot send to other people, and cannot change a notice somebody else wrote.
Notification Manager
Sends to chosen people or to everybody, edits their own notices, and sees the full delivery history — who was told, who answered, who is still waiting.

Setup

Install it, try it, in under two minutes

1
Add the module and install it
Copy it into your addons path, update the app list, then install Velkio Reminders & Notifications from Apps. It depends only on base, mail and web.
2
Send yourself a reminder
Open any screen in Odoo, create a reminder for one minute from now, and watch it land.
3
Grant the manager group
Give the people who broadcast to others the Notification Manager group, and you are fully set up.
